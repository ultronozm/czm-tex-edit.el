#+title: czm-tex-edit.el: Helper functions for editing LaTeX
#+author: Paul Nelson

* Overview
This package contains a large number of miscellaneous helper functions for editing LaTeX documents.  They're a bit complicated and personal, but I figured I'd share them in case they're of any use or inspiration.

* Configuration
This package requires [[https://www.gnu.org/software/auctex/manual/auctex/Installation.html#Installation][AUCTeX]] 14.1.0 or later and [[https://github.com/ultronozm/dynexp.el][dynexp.el]], so install those first.

Download this repository, install using =M-x package-install-file= (or package-vc-install, straight, elpaca, ...), and add something like the following to your [[https://www.emacswiki.org/emacs/InitFile][init file]]:

#+begin_src elisp
(use-package czm-tex-edit
  :after latex dynexp
  :bind
  (:map LaTeX-mode-map
        ("C-c t i" . czm-tex-edit-emphasize)
        ("C-c t a" . czm-tex-edit-alertify)
        ("C-c t b" . czm-tex-edit-bold)
        ("C-c t l" . czm-tex-edit-underline)
        ("C-c t u" . czm-tex-edit-unemphasize)
        ("C-c t e" . czm-tex-edit-external-document-link)
        ("C-c p d" . czm-tex-edit-repeat-line-contents)
        ("C-c p r" . czm-tex-edit-repeat-region)
        ("C-c p s" . czm-tex-edit-substackify)
        ("C-c p i" . czm-tex-edit-yank-interior-delete-delim)
        ("C-c p f" . czm-tex-edit-fractionify-region)
        ("C-c p b" . czm-tex-edit-enlarge-parentheses)
        ("C-c p h" . czm-tex-edit-split-equation)
        ("C-c e" . czm-tex-edit-make-equation-numbered)
        ("C-c w" . czm-tex-edit-make-equation-align)
        ("C-c q" . czm-tex-edit-make-equation-multline)
        ("s-<return>" . czm-tex-edit-return)
        ("$" . czm-tex-edit-insert-dollar-or-wrap-region)
        ("\"" . czm-tex-edit-insert-quote-or-wrap-region))
  :config
  (czm-tex-edit-define-color-functions-and-bindings
   "C-c t c"
   (("red" . "r") ("green" . "g") ("blue" . "b") ("yellow" . "y") ("orange" . "o") ("purple" . "p") ("black" . "k") ("white" . "w") ("cyan" . "c") ("magenta" . "m") ("lime" . "l") ("teal" . "t") ("violet" . "v") ("pink" . "i") ("brown" . "n") ("gray" . "a") ("darkgreen" . "d") ("lightblue" . "h") ("lavender" . "e") ("maroon" . "u") ("beige" . "j") ("indigo" . "x") ("turquoise" . "q") ("gold" . "f") ("silver" . "s") ("bronze" . "z"))))
#+end_src

Customize the binds to your liking.  Use the built-in documentation (=C-h f czm-tex-edit-whatever=) to see what the various commands do.

* Removed commands (now in AUCTeX)
The commands =czm-tex-edit-make-equation-inline= and
=czm-tex-edit-repeat-most-recent-equation= used to live in this package but
have been moved upstream to AUCTeX.  If you are using a recent AUCTeX
(e.g. 14.1.1), use these equivalents instead:

- =LaTeX-make-inline=
- =LaTeX-repeat-recent-math=

* Math environment toggles
The commands =czm-tex-edit-toggle-numbered=, =czm-tex-edit-toggle-align=,
and =czm-tex-edit-toggle-multline= use AUCTeX's =LaTeX-modify-math= to
convert the math construct at point.  The align and multline toggles
preserve numbering when switching from equation environments.  The
numbered toggle switches between =equation= and =equation*=; other
math constructs become =equation*=.  These commands do not insert labels.

* Folding inserted text
The following optional helpers fold inserted text only when =TeX-fold-mode=
is enabled.  They preserve point and do not install hooks or advice on
their own:

#+begin_src elisp
(add-hook 'LaTeX-after-insert-env-hook
          #'czm-tex-edit-fold-inserted-environment)
(advice-add 'LaTeX-insert-item :after #'czm-tex-edit-fold-previous-macro)
(advice-add 'yank :after #'czm-tex-edit-fold-yanked-references)
#+end_src

The environment helper folds the whole inserted environment, including
configured macros inside it.  The yank helper folds each =\\ref= or
=\\eqref= in the actual inserted region, including when a prefix argument
exchanges point and mark.  The item helper folds the macro before point.
If you want all macros inserted by =TeX-insert-macro= to fold automatically,
AUCTeX already provides =TeX-fold-auto=; that also covers ordinary
=LaTeX-insert-item= calls.
